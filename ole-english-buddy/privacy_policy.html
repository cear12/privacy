Privacy Policy

Ole English Buddy
Last updated: October 2026

Overview

Ole English Buddy is an English vocabulary learning app. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data the app collects and how it is used.

Data We Collect

Ole English Buddy does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Specifically:

  • No account or registration is required.
  • No name, email address, or contact information is collected.
  • No location data is collected.
  • No usage analytics or tracking is performed.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores your preferences (language, theme, selected categories) locally on your device using the standard on-device storage of your operating system (Android shared preferences or iOS user defaults). This data never leaves your device.

In-App Purchases

The app offers an optional one-time Pro upgrade via Google Play (Android) or the Apple App Store (iOS). All payment processing is handled entirely by Google Play or Apple. We do not receive or store any payment or billing information.

Third-Party Services

The app uses Google Play's in-app billing service on Android and Apple's in-app purchase service on iOS. The respective privacy policies apply to these services:

  • Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Children's Privacy

Ole English Buddy does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above.
