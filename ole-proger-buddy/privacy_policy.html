Privacy Policy

Ole Proger Buddy
Last updated: September 2026

Overview

Ole Proger Buddy is an offline programming and coding-interview practice app (flashcards, quizzes, and coding problems for C++ and other languages). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data the app collects and how it is used.

Data We Collect

Ole Proger Buddy does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Specifically:

  • No account or registration is required.
  • No name, email address, or contact information is collected.
  • No location data is collected.
  • No usage analytics or tracking is performed.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores your preferences (programming language, theme, selected categories) and study progress locally on your device using the standard Android shared preferences mechanism. This data never leaves your device.

In-App Purchases

Ole Proger Buddy does not currently offer any in-app purchases or subscriptions. If this changes in a future version, this policy will be updated first, and any billing will be processed entirely by Google Play — we would not receive or store payment or billing information.

Third-Party Services

The app makes no network requests and does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or billing services.

Children's Privacy

Ole Proger Buddy does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above.
